What we do

Sixteen Services, One Counter

Whether you are printing three hundred PE uniforms for the new term or you simply need two passport photos before noon, you can settle it all at our counter at Sand Town Junction. Here is the full list of what we handle.

Printing & Branding

Banner Design and Prints

Bold, weather-ready vinyl banners for church programs, graduations, campaigns and shop fronts.

T-Shirts and PE Uniform Print

Crisp, long-lasting prints on school PE uniforms, staff shirts and event tees — any quantity.

Face Caps and Track Suits

Branded caps and track suits that keep your team looking sharp on and off the field.

Neck Ties and Socks

Custom ties and socks finished with your school crest, church logo or company colours.

Diploma Covers

Handsome gold-foil diploma covers that give your graduates something to be proud of.

Jersey / Tap Print

Full team jerseys and taps with names, numbers and sponsor logos, pressed to last the season.

Invitations & Tickets

Weddings, anniversaries, fundraisers and gate tickets of all types — numbered on request.

Program Sheets

Service programs, funeral bulletins and event booklets of all types, laid out and printed to order.

Documents & Tech

School Stationery Supplies

Exercise books, registers, pens and record sheets supplied in bulk before the term begins.

Bound Documents

Theses, reports and proposals bound neatly with spiral, comb or hard-cover finishing.

Laminated Documents

Certificates, ID cards and price lists sealed to survive rain, dust and daily handling.

Passport-Size Photos

Clear passport and ID photos taken and printed while you wait, cut to standard size.

Photocopy Documents

Fast, clean copies from a single page to a full document set, in black-and-white or colour.

Calendars

Wall and desk calendars carrying your logo, your photos and your message for the year.

Software Installation

Windows, Office, antivirus and drivers installed properly and set up ready to use.

Computer Repairs

Laptop and desktop diagnosis, cleaning, part replacement and data recovery you can trust.

About KBC

Why People Keep Coming Back

Korkollie Business Center sits opposite ELBC Station at Sand Town Junction in Paynesville, and we have built our name on one simple habit: doing the job properly the first time. Schools trust us with their uniforms and diploma covers, churches trust us with their anniversary banners and program sheets, and business owners trust us with their signage, their documents and their computers.

We are a small team that treats every order as our own reputation on display — because it is. That is what "Best of the Best" means to us.

How it works

Three Steps, No Confusion

Ordering from us is straightforward, whether you walk in or send everything by WhatsApp.

  1. 1

    Send your design or idea

    WhatsApp us your artwork, a photo, or simply describe what you need. No design file? Our team will draw it up for you.

  2. 2

    Approve the proof

    We send you a proof and a firm price. Check the spelling, the colours and the sizing, then give us the go-ahead.

  3. 3

    Collect your finished order

    Pick up at Sand Town Junction, opposite ELBC Station — or arrange delivery for large school and church orders.
